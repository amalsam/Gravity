Electric Field simulation

Interactive electric field simulator. Place positive and negative charges and watch Coulomb field lines and equipotential lines update in real time.

What you’re looking at

An electric field visualiser built on Coulomb's law. Each charge produces a field E = kq / r², and by the superposition principle the total field at any point is the vector sum of every charge's contribution. Field lines start on positive charges and end on negative ones; equipotential lines cross them at right angles.

How to use it

  1. 1Choose positive or negative, then click or tap to place a charge.
  2. 2Toggle field lines and equipotential lines on or off.
  3. 3Clear all charges to start a new arrangement.

Questions

What is Coulomb's law?

+

The force between two point charges is F = k·q₁·q₂ / r²: proportional to the product of the charges and inversely proportional to the square of their distance. Like charges repel, opposite charges attract.

Why do field lines never cross?

+

The electric field has exactly one direction at each point. If two lines crossed, the field would point two ways at once, which is impossible.
