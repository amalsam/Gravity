Fluid SPH simulation

Real-time 2D water simulation in your browser. Break a dam, stir the fluid, pour more in and flip gravity to watch particle-based hydrodynamics splash, slosh and settle. Tune viscosity from water to honey.

What you’re looking at

A real-time 2D fluid built from particles, in the family of Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics (SPH). Each particle measures how crowded its neighbourhood is; crowded regions push particles apart and sparse ones pull them together, which produces pressure, splashes and a cohesive surface. The solver uses double density relaxation (Clavet et al., 2005), which moves particles directly to relieve pressure and so stays stable at real-time step sizes.

How to use it

  1. 1The tank opens with a dam break. Drag with the Stir tool to push the water around.
  2. 2Switch to Pour and drag to add more fluid.
  3. 3Flip gravity up, down or to zero for a big splash, and raise viscosity to turn water into oil or honey.
  4. 4Colour the particles by speed or density to see how the flow and pressure change.

Questions

What is smoothed particle hydrodynamics?

+

A mesh-free method that represents a fluid as particles. Quantities like density and pressure at any point are computed as a weighted sum over nearby particles, using a smoothing kernel that falls off with distance.

What does viscosity change?

+

Viscosity is a fluid's resistance to flow. Raising it makes neighbouring particles share velocity more strongly, so the fluid behaves more like honey than water.
