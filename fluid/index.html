Fluid SPH simulation

Real-time 2D fluid simulation using smoothed particle hydrodynamics. Pour water, change gravity and viscosity, and watch pressure and flow in your browser.

What you’re looking at

Smoothed particle hydrodynamics (SPH) models a fluid as many particles that each carry mass. A smoothing kernel turns nearby particles into a continuous density; density gives pressure, pressure gives force, and viscosity smooths out velocity differences. The result is water that splashes, settles and sloshes in real time.

How to use it

  1. 1Click or drag on the canvas to pour in fluid particles (up to 600).
  2. 2Set gravity to down, zero or up, and change the viscosity.
  3. 3Switch the colour mode between speed, density and flat.

Questions

What is smoothed particle hydrodynamics?

+

A mesh-free method that represents a fluid as particles. Quantities like density and pressure at any point are computed as a weighted sum over nearby particles, using a smoothing kernel that falls off with distance.

What does viscosity change?

+

Viscosity is a fluid's resistance to flow. Raising it makes neighbouring particles share velocity more strongly, so the fluid behaves more like honey than water.
