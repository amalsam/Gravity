Questions

What is Stacklor Lab?

+

Stacklor Lab is a free collection of interactive physics simulations — gravity, orbits, a 3D black hole, chaos, flocking, cellular automata, electric fields and fluids — that run directly in your web browser.

Are the simulations free and do they work on phones?

+

Yes. Every simulation is free, needs no account or download, and works on desktop and mobile browsers. Controls sit in a side panel on desktop and a swipe-up sheet on phones.

Are the simulations physically accurate?

+

Each one is built from the real governing equations — Newton's law of gravitation, Coulomb's law, the Lorenz equations, smoothed particle hydrodynamics — integrated numerically in real time. Some visual effects, like black hole lensing, are approximations chosen so they run smoothly in a browser.

Can I use these simulations for teaching?

+

Yes. Every simulation has a The science panel that explains the equations behind it, which makes them useful for classroom demonstrations and self-study in physics, maths and computer science.
